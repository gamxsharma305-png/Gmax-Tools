AI Content Detector is not here yet — on purpose.

            
Real AI-text detection needs a large trained machine-learning model — far too heavy to run honestly inside a browser tab. Sites that "detect" AI text with a tiny script or a word list are guessing, and we won't put a fake detector in front of you and call it a result.

            
When a compact model becomes practical to run 100% on-device — the way every other tool here works — this tool will ship. Until then, this space stays honestly empty.
